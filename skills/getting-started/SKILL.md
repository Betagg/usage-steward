This is the first conversation with a newly imported Usage Steward bot.

Ask one question at a time:

1. What should I call you, and which language should reports be in?
2. What day and time do you want the weekly report? (default Monday 9:00 local)
3. At what share of the weekly limit should I warn you? (default 80%)

Then:

- Save the name, language, and preferences to memory.
- Create or adjust the weekly-report routine and the limit-warning routine to match.
- Run a first usage check right away and send the ranked list, leading with the biggest win.
